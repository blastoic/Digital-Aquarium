Behind
the Water

Digital Aquarium is a study of how digital art, visual storytelling and interactive web experiences can share one calm surface.

  • UI/UX and visual research
  • Typography and art direction
  • AI-assisted image generation
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Interactive motion
  • Responsive design

  1. Research

    Nishikigoi patterns, ukiyo-e water, and editorial posters.

  2. Design

    Oversized type, a vermilion field and one fish for scale.

  3. Development

    Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, built to load fast.

  4. Experimentation

    Parallax and drift tuned until the motion felt like water.
